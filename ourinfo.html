FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about AUTORA pre-owned vehicle audits, direct purchase workflow, doorstep delivery, and 7-day money-back guarantee.

How does the AUTORA 96-Point Inspection guarantee work?
Every vehicle on AUTORA undergoes a mandatory 96-point diagnostic assessment conducted by certified master mechanics at our regional hubs. We inspect engine cylinder compression, OBD-II error codes, underbody disc/pad wear, paint thickness gauges, and registry legal records. Any car failing frame integrity or flood damage checks is automatically barred from listing.
Can I request a doorstep test drive or hub inspection?
Yes! You can reserve any vehicle online and choose between visiting one of our 4 regional hub facilities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune) or requesting doorstep test-drive delivery directly to your home address.
What is the 7-Day No-Questions-Asked Return Policy?
We provide a 100% money-back guarantee for 7 days or up to 300 KM driven post-delivery. If you find any dissatisfaction with your purchased vehicle, simply initiate a return via your account or customer care for a full refund.
Are there any hidden dealer commission fees?
None. The price displayed on AUTORA is 100% transparent and inclusive of standard inspection certification, documentation transfer assistance, and doorstep delivery estimation.
How do I get my car audited or listed on AUTORA?
Sellers can schedule an on-site audit at any of our 4 regional hubs or request a mobile inspection unit. Once the 96-point assessment is complete, your vehicle gets a verified digital certificate and goes live on AUTORA's marketplace.
How is nationwide doorstep delivery coordinated?
AUTORA operates a dedicated fleet of covered flatbed transporters. Once digital escrow clearing is completed, your vehicle is loaded onto a covered trailer and delivered straight to your driveway with live GPS tracking.
